Research

Explore cryptographic ideas without overstating what has been proven.

Adaptive Hashing Encryption (AHE) is experimental work exploring layered encryption/API design and post-quantum components. It should be evaluated through explicit algorithm choices, key and nonce handling, test vectors, interoperability, benchmarking, and independent cryptographic review. This portfolio does not claim AHE is independently proven secure or quantum-safe.

Implementation evidence

Code, dependency versions, API behavior, round-trip tests, and reproducible examples.

Security review

Threat model, key management, misuse resistance, side-channel considerations, and expert review.
